3. The Recommended Fix Trigger instant UPI refunds upon doorstep pickup scan and enforce 2D barcode verification at warehouse packing stations.

Summary of Key Findings (Presentation Talking Points)

  • Post-pickup refund trap (34%): More than a third of return complaints happen after the item was already successfully collected. Customers wait 10+ days for UPI refunds or receive unwanted store credits.
  • Warehouse errors exceed sizing by 3.4x: Sizing accounts for only 7% of return complaints, while fulfillment errors (wrong size tags, mismatched items sent by sellers) account for 24%.
  • Logistics handshake breakdown (1.05 / 5): Delivery agent no-shows, false "Customer declined" app updates, and courier refusal to collect mismatched tags create the lowest customer satisfaction.
  • Noise accurately filtered: 289 reviews (74.3%) were positive praise or general shopping comments caught by keyword filters, cleanly isolated under "Other".
